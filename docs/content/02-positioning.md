## The one thing
**"We take out only what you don't want — and look after the part you keep."**
No Melbourne competitor explains how the retained structure is protected (cut line,
propping, weather-sealing the open end, services abolished not just switched off), or
writes the job around the next trade's start date. Metro's name already says it.

## Voice
Plain, practical, straight-talking. First person "we". Short sentences, tradie words the
buyer uses ("rip out", "back to the frame", "tip run", "make good"). No hype, no "leading".

## Facts to correct in any copy (from the fact-check)
- Say "registered with the Building and Plumbing Commission (BPC)", never "VBA".
- Services are **abolished** before demolition (meter and supply removed), not just disconnected.
- No "pre-2003 asbestos register" rule in Victoria; the contractor must identify and remove
  asbestos before demolition, so far as practicable.
- Non-friable asbestos: licence needed over 10 m² **or** over 1 hour of removal in 7 days.
- Council hard rubbish excludes building rubble, asbestos and gas bottles.
